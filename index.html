About

Information Technology undergraduate at Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember, Surabaya. The work so far sits between data systems, models, and the screen in front of them.

In the GoTo Dicoding cohort I built VendorAI, a vendor recommender for small businesses: a React and Tailwind interface, a FastAPI backend, and PostgreSQL underneath, with a generated explanation for each ranked vendor. Before that I interned as a quality engineer at Telkom Infrastruktur Indonesia, checking network-asset records against what was actually in the field.

I also built a Steam game lakehouse (Kafka, PySpark, MinIO, then a Streamlit dashboard), contributed to Licky, an Android app that reads tongue photos on-device with TensorFlow Lite, and fine-tuned DistilBERT for multilabel emotion classification.

Interested in data systems, machine learning, and how a model shows up in a product.
